1 · Share your slides

Works with PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Canva or a PDF. When asked, pick the tab or window with your slides (or the projector screen if you have two). Pulse only sends while the shared tab or window fills the whole screen, which keeps the normal editor off phones. That check is best effort: anything else you make full screen in that tab (notes, another app) would be sent too, so share only the slideshow and use Pause before showing anything private.
