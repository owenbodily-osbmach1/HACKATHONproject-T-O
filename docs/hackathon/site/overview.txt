 02 / Sponsors and ideas
 Who you're building with
 Hover for ideas 
 AI code review · Track 1
 Gitar is Sonar's AI code verification agent. It reviews your pull requests against your project's conventions, finds problems, generates fixes and diagnoses CI failures. With auto-apply turned on, it commits fixes and keeps iterating against your pipeline, while you stay in control of what gets applied and what can merge.
 Ideas for Sonar
 A schedule builder where every feature lands through a Gitar-reviewed PR, with review instructions that make sure session times always handle time zones properly. 
 A repository rule that kicks in whenever your API changes and checks your frontend still matches it. 
 Breaking your CI on purpose and letting auto-apply fix it live during your demo.

 Ideas for Dash0
 Self-diagnosing microservice app. Build a small multi-service app (a storefront, a booking system, a chat app) and instrument it end to end with OpenTelemetry. Add a "chaos" button that injects latency or errors, then show Agent0 finding the cause in your demo. 
 A live crowd map where check-ins become metrics and a Dash0 dashboard shows which rooms are filling up. 
 A schedule app instrumented end to end, where your demo shows Agent0 explaining why "My agenda" slowed down when everyone opened it after the keynote. 
 An AI assistant for attendees traced with OpenLIT, so you can see every LLM call, token and cost in Dash0. 
 Hover for ideas 
 Observability · Track 2
 Dash0 is an observability platform built natively on OpenTelemetry, so everything your applications and infrastructure emit can be explored together instead of across separate tools. Ask Agent0, our AI SRE agent, a question in plain English and get an answer grounded in your live telemetry. Its MCP server lets Claude Code, Cursor and other AI assistants tap into the same data while you build, so they work from facts instead of guesses.

 Kubernetes UI · Track 3
 Radar is an open-source Kubernetes UI from Skyhook. It shows everything running in your cluster in one place, and its AI SRE finds the root cause when something breaks, either from the Radar UI or from your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others) through Radar's MCP server.
 Ideas for Radar, any of these on Kubernetes
 Talk picker . Reads your GitHub, builds your schedule and reshuffles it when a room fills up. 
 Hallway track matchmaker . Finds the people in the building working on the same problem as you. 
 Live crowd map . Shows which rooms are packed and which coffee line is shortest.

 Search and vector database · Track 4
 Elasticsearch is the open source search engine and vector database. Store your app's data in it and search it by keyword, by meaning with vector search, or both at once with hybrid search, so it can be the database behind your search, recommendations and the RAG for your AI agents.

 05 / FAQ
 Questions, answered
 Is it in person or online? In person only, at The Shop Workspace in downtown Salt Lake City on Monday, November 9. We can't cover travel or accommodation.
 Who can take part? Everyone is welcome, whether it's your first hackathon or your fiftieth. Register and come along.
 Can I enter more than one track? Yes. Your project enters every sponsor track whose tool it genuinely uses, and the Merge Forward track as well. Every project is in the running for best overall.
 Do I need a team? No. Come on your own or with a team. There's time at kickoff to find people to build with.
 What should I bring? Your laptop and charger. Set up your Gitar, Dash0 and Radar Cloud accounts before you arrive, and have Docker and a Kubernetes cluster ready if you're entering the Elasticsearch track. The Radar track just needs a GitHub account and a coding agent.
 Is food provided? Yes. Breakfast at 10:00 AM, lunch at 2:00 PM and coffee through the day.
 Does everyone really get a keyboard? Yes. Everyone who attends in person gets a Keychron mechanical keyboard, plus $50 in OpenAI credits, swag and a certificate.
 How do I get into the After-Party? Sign up for House of Kube on Luma. It's invite only, but your registration is confirmed right away and the venue address shows up once you sign up. Entry is free.
